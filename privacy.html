Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information PianoVerse ("we", "us") collects when you use our website and web app, why we collect it, and the choices you have. The service is operated by [Company legal name], [registered address]. Contact: hello@yourdomain.com.

1. The short version

  • You can use the piano without creating an account.
  • Your settings, stars, streaks and custom songs are stored in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers.
  • Analytics run only if you accept them in our cookie banner.
  • If you contact us or join our newsletter, we use your details only for that purpose.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we collect

Stored on your device

The app saves preferences (theme, sound, labels), learning progress (stars, practice minutes, streaks) and any songs you add in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device; you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data or using "Reset progress" in the app settings.

Analytics (optional)

If you accept analytics, we use [Google Analytics 4 / Plausible Analytics] to understand how visitors use the site (for example, pages viewed and buttons clicked). [If GA4: IP addresses are anonymised. If Plausible: no cookies are set and no personal data is collected.] You can change your choice at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Forms

When you use our contact form we receive your name, email address, subject and message. When you subscribe to our newsletter we receive your email address. These are processed by [Formspree / your form provider] and [Mailchimp / ConvertKit / Brevo] on our behalf.

Third-party resources

To load fonts and realistic piano sounds, your browser may request files from Google Fonts and from the piano sample host. These providers may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.

MIDI devices

If you connect a MIDI keyboard, note data is processed entirely inside your browser and is never sent to us.

3. Why we use information

  • To provide and improve the service
  • To reply to your messages
  • To send newsletters you asked for (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse

Our legal bases are: your consent (analytics, newsletter), our legitimate interests (security, improving the service) and, where applicable, performance of a contract (paid plans).

7. Children

PianoVerse can be used by children without providing any personal information. Children under [13/16, depending on your jurisdiction] should not submit forms or subscribe to the newsletter without a parent or guardian's permission.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email hello@yourdomain.com. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. Significant changes will be highlighted on the website.
