Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll help.

Getting started

Do I need to download anything?

No. PianoVerse runs in your web browser. Just open the app and start playing. If you like, you can install it to your phone's home screen or your computer like a regular app.

Do I need a real piano or keyboard?

No. Play with your fingers on a touchscreen, with a mouse, or with your computer keyboard: A S D F G H J K are white keys, and W E T Y U O P are black keys. Z and X shift the octave, and the space bar works as the sustain pedal.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required to play. Your settings, stars and streaks are saved in your browser on that device.

How do I learn my first song?

Open the app, tap Songs, choose a Beginner song such as Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, and press the key that glows gold. In Step-by-step mode the song waits for you at every note.

Learning

I can't read sheet music. Can I still learn?

Yes. The next key glows on the keyboard and its name appears in the notation you choose. A small staff also shows each note, so you gradually learn to read music without pressure.

What notation systems are supported?

Four: Western letters (C D E), Indian Sargam (Sa Re Ga, with komal and tivra notes marked), Solfège (Do Re Mi) and the PC-keyboard key to press. The step panel shows all of them at once.

What are the practice modes?

Listen plays the song while keys light up. Step-by-step waits for you to find each note. Play-along keeps tempo after a count-in and scores each note as Perfect, Good or Miss. You can also slow down to 40%, practise phrase by phrase and loop sections. A Memory mode is on the way.

Can I learn Indian and Bollywood-style music?

Yes. The Hindi / Indian tab includes Sargam basics, Raag Yaman and traditional songs and bhajans in simplified teaching arrangements. You can set any key as Sa. We only include public-domain and traditional music, so current film songs are not in the library.

How long until I can play a song?

Most beginners finish a simple song like Mary Had a Little Lamb in their first session. Steady progress comes from short daily practice of 10 to 15 minutes.

Pricing

Is PianoVerse free?

Yes. The piano, starter songs and first lessons are free forever. Pro adds the full library and advanced tools. See pricing.

What do I get with Pro?

The full song library, all practice modes, all lesson levels, recording and unlimited custom songs. Some Pro features are still rolling out, and the pricing page marks them clearly.

Do you offer school or teacher plans?

Yes. The School / Teacher plan includes multiple student accounts and a progress dashboard. Contact us for classroom pricing.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like. You keep paid features until the end of the billing period, then return to the Free plan with your progress intact.

Technical

Which devices and browsers are supported?

Any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone and iPad. On phones, hold your device in landscape to see more keys.

How do I connect a MIDI keyboard?

Connect your keyboard by USB (or a USB/Bluetooth MIDI adapter), open Settings in the app and choose Connect MIDI. Web MIDI works in Chrome and Edge; Safari doesn't support it yet. Your sustain pedal works too.

I don't hear any sound. What should I do?

Tap a key once: browsers only allow sound after your first touch or click. Check that your device isn't on silent (on iPhone, turn off the side mute switch) and that the volume in the app's settings is up.

Does it work offline?

Once you've opened PianoVerse online, it can be installed and will open without a connection. The realistic piano recordings load from the internet the first time; offline, a built-in synthesized piano takes over automatically.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, in your browser's local storage on that device. Clearing your browser data or using private browsing will reset it. Account sync across devices is coming soon.
