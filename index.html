FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a real piano or keyboard?

No. You can play with your fingers on a phone or tablet, with a mouse, or with your computer keyboard (A S D F G H J K are the white keys). If you have a MIDI keyboard, plug it in and PianoVerse will use it.

Is PianoVerse really free?

Yes. The piano, starter songs and first lessons are free. Pro adds the full library and advanced tools for people who want more.

I can't read sheet music. Can I still learn?

Absolutely. That's who PianoVerse is built for. The next key glows on the keyboard and its name appears in the notation you choose. A small staff shows the same note so you learn to read gradually, without pressure.

What is Sargam and why does it matter?

Sargam (Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni) is the note system of Indian classical and film music. Many learners in South Asia already know it from singing, so PianoVerse lets you learn piano in Sargam, with komal and tivra notes clearly marked.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. PianoVerse runs in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, Mac and Linux. On phones, turn your device sideways to see more keys. You can also install it to your home screen.
