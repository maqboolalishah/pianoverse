Values

What we stand for

Free at the core

The piano, starter songs and first lessons will always be free. Money should never be the reason someone can't start.

Every musical language

Western notation isn't the only way to read music. We respect Sargam, Solfège and simply "press this key".

Joy over pressure

Encouragement beats criticism. We celebrate small wins and make mistakes feel like part of learning.

Privacy by default

No account needed to play. Your progress lives in your browser, and analytics only run if you say yes.

Made with care

We're a small, independent team of musicians and engineers. If you have ideas, feedback or want to partner with us, we'd love to hear from you.
