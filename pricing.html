Pricing FAQ

Questions about plans

Is the Free plan really free forever?

Yes. The piano, starter songs and first lessons stay free. No credit card is needed and nothing expires.

How does yearly billing work?

Yearly plans are paid once a year and cost 20% less than paying monthly for twelve months. The price shown per month on the yearly toggle is the effective monthly cost.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. You can cancel whenever you like and keep your paid features until the end of the period you've paid for. After that, you move back to the Free plan and keep your progress.

Do you offer discounts for students or schools?

The School plan is designed for classrooms. For larger schools, non-profits or special circumstances, contact us and we'll work something out.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Paid plans will be processed by a secure payment provider supporting major cards. Schools can request invoice billing.
