Curriculum

The beginner roadmap: eight levels, one skill at a time.

Each level teaches one new idea and practises it with real songs you already know. Move at your own pace and replay any level whenever you like.

Good to know: every song listed below is already playable in the app. Interactive, guided lesson pages for each level are on the way. Coming soon
  1. 1
    Level 1 · Week 1

    Meet the keyboard

    Sit with good posture, curve your fingers, and learn finger numbers 1–5. Find the groups of two and three black keys, and use them to locate every C, including middle C.

    Goal: find any note name on the keyboard in under five seconds.

    PostureFinger numbersMiddle CSong: Sargam Basics
  2. 2
    Level 2 · Weeks 2–3

    First melodies in C position

    Place your right thumb on C and play five-finger melodies without moving your hand. Learn to keep a steady pulse with the metronome.

    Goal: play two complete songs with three stars.

    Five-finger positionSteady pulseMary Had a Little LambMachli Jal Ki Rani Hai
  3. 3
    Level 3 · Weeks 4–5

    Reading notes four ways

    Connect Western letters, Sargam, Solfège and the treble staff. Learn lines and spaces, and read simple melodies before you play them.

    Goal: name any note from C4 to G5 on the staff.

    Treble staffSargam ↔ WesternTwinkle TwinkleAloo Kachaloo
  4. 4
    Level 4 · Weeks 6–7

    Rhythm & counting

    Quarter, half, whole and dotted notes; 2/4, 3/4 and 4/4 time. Move from Step-by-step to Play-along mode and play in tempo.

    Goal: 80%+ accuracy in Play-along at 80% speed.

    Note valuesTime signaturesLondon BridgeHappy Birthday
  5. 5
    Level 5 · Weeks 8–9

    Moving beyond five fingers

    Shift your hand smoothly, pass the thumb under, and handle leaps without looking down for too long.

    Goal: play a song that spans more than an octave.

    Hand shiftsThumb-underOde to JoyWhen the Saints
  6. 6
    Level 6 · Weeks 10–11

    Black keys, sharps & raags

    Sharps and flats in Western music, komal and tivra notes in Sargam. Explore the G major scale and Raag Yaman's tivra Ma.

    Goal: play Raag Yaman's aaroh and avroh from memory.

    Sharps & flatsKomal & tivraMinuet in GRaag Yaman
  7. 7
    Level 7 · Weeks 12–14

    Both hands together

    Start with simple bass notes in the left hand, then combine hands slowly using hands-separate practice and phrase looping.

    Goal: play a two-hand piece at 70% speed.

    Left-hand bassHands-separate practiceCanon in D (simplified)
  8. 8
    Level 8 · Weeks 15–16

    Expression & performance

    Dynamics (soft and loud), legato, the sustain pedal and phrasing. Prepare one piece to perform for friends or family.

    Goal: perform a full piece with expression and three stars.

    DynamicsSustain pedalFür Elise (opening)Greensleeves
How we teach

Built on how people actually learn music

Play first, theory second

You play a real melody in your first five minutes. Theory is introduced the moment you need it, not before.

Short daily sessions

Ten focused minutes a day beat one long weekly session. Streaks and stars make daily practice a habit.

Mistakes are welcome

Wrong notes get a helpful hint, never a buzzer. Slow down, loop the hard bar, and try again.

Start Level 1 today

Open the piano, choose Sargam Basics or Twinkle Twinkle, and follow the glowing keys.
